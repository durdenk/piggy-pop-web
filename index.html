How to play

Easy to pick.
Fun to plan.

The cube picture sits at the top, the piglets wait in the tray below. Clear every cube without filling up your slots.

1

Pick a piglet

Tap the top piglet of a tray column. It hops into the first free slot.

2

Watch it blast

The piglet shoots every cube of its color it can reach on the front of the model. Empty a pillar, and the one behind it slides forward.

3

Keep slots free

A piglet leaves once its shots are used up. Fill every slot with piglets whose color is still buried, and you are stuck.

+

Plan your picks. The right order matters: a piglet whose color is hidden deep in the model only waits in its slot. Free the front first, and the rest of the picture opens up.

Power-ups

A little help
when you need it.

Stuck on a tricky picture? Four power-ups can turn a level around.

+

Extra Slot

Opens one more slot for the rest of the level.

↑

Magnet

Pulls any waiting piglet out of the tray, even from deep in a column.

↺

Send Back

Sends a piglet back to the tray with the shots it has left, so its slot is free again.

✦

Blast

A piglet fires all its shots at once at its color, even at cubes buried deep in the model.

Win levels to earn coins, then spend them on power-ups.
