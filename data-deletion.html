Delete your Hog Wash data

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This page explains how to delete data related to Hog Wash, the Android game by Cukka Creatives. Hog Wash has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete: your progress lives on your phone, and only a small amount of data leaves it. You can delete most of it yourself, and you can ask us to delete the rest.

1. Delete the data on your phone

Your progress, coins, power-ups, lives, settings and purchased items are saved only on your device. To delete them:

  • Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Hog Wash → Storage and tap Clear storage (or Clear data), or
  • Uninstall Hog Wash.

This removes your progress at once and cannot be undone. If Android backup is turned on, a copy may stay in your Google account backup until you remove it there. Purchases are kept by Google Play: Premium's lasting perks come back after a reinstall, but coins and power-ups do not.

2. Ask us to delete your data

To ask us to delete data that we control:

  1. Email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject "Hog Wash Data Deletion Request".
  2. Tell us what you want deleted. For feedback you sent from the game, add the approximate date and a few words from your message so we can find it.
  3. Do not send payment card details, passwords or other sensitive information.

We reply to confirm your request and delete the data within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Feedback messages you sent from the game's Settings, together with the random feedback ID and app version sent with them.
  • Analytics data held by GameAnalytics on our behalf. It is tied to a random install ID that we cannot link to you by name or email. Where we can identify your data, we ask GameAnalytics to delete it; otherwise it is removed under the GameAnalytics Data Retention and Deletion Policy.
  • Emails you sent us, including your deletion request, once the request is handled.

4. What is kept

  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play as long as tax, accounting and fraud-prevention laws require. We do not store them on a server of our own.

5. Data controlled by Google

Advertising data collected through Google AdMob, Google Play reviews and Google Play purchase records are controlled by Google. You can manage them yourself:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path depends on your phone).
  • Change your ad consent choices with Privacy choices in the game's Settings, where it is shown.
  • Edit or delete your review on the Hog Wash page in Google Play.
  • Manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
